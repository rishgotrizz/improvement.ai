HOW EDUPREDICT WORKS

From Academic Data to Actionable Insight

EduPredict transforms everyday academic records into a prediction, an explanation, and practical next steps.

01
Academic Data

Attendance, study hours, assessments, quizzes, previous performance and backlog information.

02
Feature Preparation

Raw academic records are converted into the nine model-ready indicators used by EduPredict.

03
ML Prediction

Two trained models estimate academic score and classify the student's current risk level.

04
Explain the Result

Feature-level contributions show which academic factors are influencing the prediction.

05
Prioritize Action

Deterministic academic rules identify the areas that need attention first.

06
Track Progress

Students can act on recommendations while faculty can monitor progress and interventions over time.

SYSTEM VALUE

ONE INPUT. FOUR LAYERS OF INSIGHT.

1. Prediction "Where could performance land?"
2. Risk "How urgent is the situation?"
3. Explanation "What's influencing the result?"
4. Action "What should improve next?"
