Ethics in Artificial Intelligence (EAI)

Responsible AI principles: Data privacy, explainability, fairness disclaimers, limitations, and human oversight

Pillar 1 Data Privacy Minimal academic data collection
Pillar 2 Explainability Transparent feature weights
Pillar 3 Fairness Unbiased feature selection
Pillar 4 Human Control Decision-support tool only

1. Data Privacy & Minimization

EduPredict enforces strict data minimization by collecting only essential academic performance metrics (e.g., attendance percentage, assignment averages, study hours). No sensitive personally identifiable information (PII) like addresses, phone numbers, or financial details is requested or stored.

2. Model Explainability

Instead of opaque deep learning models, EduPredict employs interpretable linear regression and logistic classification models. Every prediction presents exact feature contribution weights so students understand why a specific risk status was estimated.

3. Fairness & Demographic Disclaimer

"The current dataset does not contain demographic or protected subgroup attributes for a meaningful subgroup fairness evaluation."
Model training relies strictly on academic metrics (attendance, study hours, assignment marks) to prevent algorithmic discrimination based on sensitive protected traits.

4. Model Limitations & Human Oversight

  • Predictions depend directly on historical sample data quality.
  • Correlation discovered during EDA does not imply direct causation.
  • EduPredict is a decision-support advisor; faculty and students retain final authority.
  • Students should never be labeled or judged solely by model output estimates.
